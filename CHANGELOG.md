## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added

- Terminal tabs open again when the IDE restarts.
- The sidebar selects the row of the active terminal tab. View Options > Always Select Opened Terminal turns it off.
- The `herdr-attach` helper for Intel macOS and Linux (x86_64 and aarch64). Kitty graphics do not show as base64 text on these platforms.

### Fixed

- Split of a terminal tab moves the tab instead of showing the terminal twice.
